Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

This notice describes how the LivingPhotos app for Apple TV and this website handle information. The app uses the Apple Photos library available on your Apple TV, including photos synchronized through iCloud Photos if you use that Apple service.

2. Visiting this website

The website is hosted as a static site using GitHub Pages, provided by GitHub, Inc. When the site is requested, technical connection information (including IP address, request date and time, requested content, and browser or device information) may be processed to deliver the site and protect the hosting infrastructure. GitHub states that visitors’ IP addresses are logged and stored for security purposes.

The legal basis for the processing necessary to provide and protect the website is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR (our legitimate interest in a functional and secure website). GitHub determines the storage periods for its operational and security logs; we do not set those periods. Further information: GitHub Pages documentation and GitHub Privacy Statement.

Our website does not use its own analytics software, advertising trackers, contact forms, or embedded third-party fonts. We do not intentionally set tracking cookies. It links to external websites only when you choose to follow a link.

3. Contact by email

If you email us, we process the email address, message content, and any information you voluntarily provide to respond to your request. Our email provider processes the messages for technical delivery and storage. The applicable legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR for contractual or pre-contractual enquiries, or Article 6(1)(f) GDPR for general support and correspondence. We retain messages for as long as needed to resolve the enquiry, unless applicable legal retention duties require longer storage.

4. Access to your photo library

LivingPhotos accesses the Apple Photos library only after the photo-library permission required by the operating system has been granted. The app uses photos, videos, album and folder information, media identifiers, timestamps, favorite status and, where available, photo location metadata to provide its features, including Time Travel, Places, Events, search, slideshows, zoom and photo management.

When you rotate a photo, change its favorite status, or confirm deletion, the app makes the requested change through Apple's Photos APIs. If you use iCloud Photos, changes to the Photos library may synchronize across your Apple devices. LivingPhotos does not upload your entire photo library or original photos to a server operated by us.

Access is controlled through the Apple TV operating system. If permission is withdrawn, the photo-library features may no longer work.

5. Data stored locally on Apple TV

To make navigation and analysis possible, the app can create local photo indexes and caches, thumbnails, event and similarity results, photo identifiers, viewing history, map/place-name caches and preferences. Preview images used by the Apple TV Top Shelf may also be stored in the app's local shared storage. Similarity analysis creates derived visual fingerprints of photos; this is used to compare images, not to identify people.

These records are stored within the app's local storage or cache areas. Some caches may be removed automatically by tvOS. Uninstalling the app generally removes its local app data, subject to Apple's platform behavior; it does not delete the underlying Photos library.

6. iCloud and CloudKit metadata

When an iCloud account is available, LivingPhotos uses Apple's private CloudKit database associated with that account to synchronize selected app data. This includes display preferences and recently visited months, albums, places and photo identifiers; recent place records can include coordinates. The app also backs up its similarity-analysis database to private CloudKit storage when backup is due and the app is active. That database contains photo identifiers, derived image fingerprints and similarity values; it is not an upload of the original photographs.

These data are processed by Apple as part of its iCloud services, not stored on our own servers. If iCloud is unavailable, the app can continue using locally available data. CloudKit data may persist independently of local app caches and may remain in iCloud after the app is uninstalled. iCloud storage and removal are governed by your Apple account settings and Apple's services. For questions about data stored by LivingPhotos, contact us at the email above.

7. Places and Apple Maps

For the Places feature, photo location coordinates can be passed to Apple's MapKit and reverse-geocoding services to obtain place names and display maps. Apple may process those requests under its own privacy terms. This concerns the locations recorded in photos, not necessarily the current physical location of your Apple TV. See Apple Maps & Privacy.

8. Trial and in-app purchases

The free trial and full-version purchase are handled using Apple's StoreKit and App Store services. Apple processes payment information. LivingPhotos checks transaction and entitlement information required to enable purchased features and maintain access. We do not receive payment card details. See Apple Privacy Policy.

9. Advertising, tracking and automated decisions

LivingPhotos does not use a proprietary advertising network or third-party analytics SDK, and it does not track users across other companies' apps or websites. Automated image similarity grouping is used for organizing photos; we do not conduct automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects on individuals.

10. Retention and choices

Local caches are retained as needed for the app and may be purged by the system. Recently viewed history can be cleared in the app's settings; this also triggers synchronization of the cleared history when CloudKit is available. Other CloudKit records, including similarity backups and settings, may remain in the private iCloud database until they are removed through the available Apple/iCloud data controls. Removing the app does not itself erase your iCloud Photos library or necessarily erase its CloudKit records.

Sharing access to the photo library is optional, but required for the corresponding app features. No email contact is required to use the app. If you would like help understanding or exercising deletion options, contact us.

11. Recipients and international transfers

Depending on use, personal data may be processed by GitHub (website hosting), Apple (Photos, iCloud/CloudKit, Maps and StoreKit) and our email service provider (support messages). GitHub and Apple may process data outside the European Economic Area. Their published privacy notices describe international transfers and safeguards such as EU Standard Contractual Clauses and, where applicable, recognized adequacy frameworks. See GitHub Privacy Statement and Apple Privacy Policy.

12. Your rights

Where the GDPR applies, you may have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection to the processing of your personal data, subject to legal requirements. You may contact us at livingphotos@strouhal-hoferichter.de. You may also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular the Hessischer Beauftragter für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit.

We may update this policy if the app, website or services change. The version published here is the current version.
