2. Apple Photos access

With your permission, LivingPhotos accesses your Apple Photos library to display photos, videos and metadata such as dates, albums and locations for its browsing, Time Travel, Places and Events features.

3. Data stored by the app

Depending on the enabled features, LivingPhotos may store photo indexes, thumbnails, settings, recent activity and generated metadata locally or via Apple's iCloud/CloudKit. LivingPhotos is not an independent photo-hosting service. Please verify the final scope against the released app.

4. In-app purchases

Trials and purchases use Apple's StoreKit. Payment details are processed by Apple; the app receives transaction and entitlement information for access control.

5. Website hosting

This website is intended to be hosted on GitHub Pages, a service provided by GitHub, Inc. When you access the website, the hosting provider may process necessary technical connection information such as IP address, request time and browser data. Retention periods, applicable legal bases and any international transfers require final review.

6. Cookies and analytics

This static website does not deliberately embed advertising, analytics trackers, external fonts or tracking cookies. Hosting-side processing may still apply.

7. Your rights

Under applicable data protection law, you may have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection and complaint to a supervisory authority. Please contact livingphotos@strouhal-hoferichter.de.

We may update this notice as the website or app evolves.
