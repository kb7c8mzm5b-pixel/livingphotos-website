Support

For questions about LivingPhotos, installation, photo access, the trial or your purchase, please email livingphotos@strouhal-hoferichter.de.

Frequently asked questions

Where are my photos stored?

LivingPhotos displays content from your Apple Photos library. It does not operate a separate photo hosting service.

What happens after the three-day trial?

You can purchase the full version once to continue using the app. This is not a subscription.

How do I restore a purchase?

Use the purchase restoration option in the app with the Apple Account originally used for the purchase.
